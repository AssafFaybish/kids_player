# v1.0.26 — what changed, and why

Five changes: three features and two fixes, each of which turned out to be more than one bug. The enforcing
detail lives in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); this is the map. For how to verify any of it, see
[TESTING.md](TESTING.md). Previous release: [V1025.md](V1025.md).

| # | Change | PR |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deleting a profile actually deletes its data — **and it stays deleted** | [#47](https://github.com/devfassaf/kids_player/pull/47) |
| 2 | A **playlist** is a source (the "link not supported" that kept coming back) | [#48](https://github.com/devfassaf/kids_player/pull/48) |

## 1 · Profile deletion was a no-op, twice over

The confirm has always said *"כל הסרטונים של הפרופיל יימחקו. פעולה זו אינה הפיכה"*.
`deleteCurrentProfile` removed the row from the Preferences profile list and nothing else —
**`db.purgeProfile` existed with zero callers.** Measured: a throwaway profile with one
channel kept all 500 videos, its subscription and its sources record.

The second half is worse. Both merge paths union profiles by id and **neither filtered
them**, so deleting a child lasted exactly until another device's document arrived and put
it back. "Irreversible" was the opposite of true.

- **What may be erased is a decision** — pure `plan.planProfilePurge`. `prof:<id>` always;
  the library scope only when no sibling reads it, because `lib:<fnv1a(sheet)>` is SHARED
  and erasing it would take a sibling's whole library. A sheet-less profile owns
  `lib:p:<id>` outright, and that is where nearly all its content lives — which is why the
  old personal-scope-only purge was effectively a no-op.
- **A deletion must survive the next pull** — a grow-only tombstone map, carried in the Drive
  doc as an additive key, filtered in `mergeProfileLists`, and adopted from peers by
  `applyRemoteDoc`. Grow-only is safe here where the video deny-list needed revocation: a
  profile id is random and never reused.
- Per-library `meta` keys are **deleted**, not written as `null` — `putMeta(k, null)` leaves
  the row behind. The first version of the fix did exactly that and the browser showed it.

## 2 · A playlist is a source

Reported from the field repeatedly as *"the link is not supported"*. **The classifier was
never at fault** — `classifySourceRow` has answered `{kind:'playlist', playlistId}` since
v1.0.12, for `m.youtube.com` and `www` alike. The app then threw it away: `parentAdd`
handled only `'video'` and `'channel'`, and `parseSourceRows` pushed playlist rows into
`invalid` as `'playlist-unsupported-yet'`.

**A missing feature wearing the costume of a parse error** — and the message listed what IS
supported instead of naming what was not, so there was nothing to act on.

- Stored as a **subscription in the same table** as a channel (`libraryChannels`,
  `kind:'playlist'`). No schema change, no `DB_VERSION` bump; it inherits Drive sync,
  deletion, the parent's list and the auto-approve flag for free.
- **The unify rule** (parent's decision): a playlist video whose owner channel is also
  subscribed lands in the channel's folder. Duplicate *records* were never the risk —
  `planMutations` keys on `yt:<videoId>` — the **folder** was: `folderId` is in
  `DIFF_FIELDS`, so two passes that disagreed would rewrite the record every sync. It is
  order-free and self-healing.
- Unlike a channel's own playlists tab, **foreign videos are the point** here, and Shorts
  cannot be identified (no `UUSH` sibling exists for an arbitrary playlist), so they are
  included per the standing "an unknown signal means include" rule.

## 3 · The preview bubble

Tapping a row's **thumbnail** in ממתינים or in הוספה opens a floating player over the parent
screen. An overlay, never a view: scroll position, open tab and ticked rows all survive.

- **Not the kid player, deliberately.** `setupHud` binds window/document listeners and must
  never run twice without a teardown; the kid HUD also hides the timeline and turns a centre
  tap into play/pause — backwards for someone jumping through a video to judge it. The
  bubble builds a plain iframe from pure `playerlogic.previewEmbedUrl`: `controls=1`,
  `mute=1`, `rel=0`. All three fail silently when wrong, so all three are pinned.
- A decision **advances to the next item** rather than closing. A handler returning `false`
  (a cancelled delete confirm) means "nothing happened" and does not skip the video.
- **`refreshPendingList` now keeps the ticks.** It cleared the whole selection on every
  rebuild, which would have undone twenty ticks each time the parent decided one video. The
  old reason (a tick could point at a row that is gone) is answered by filtering against the
  rebuilt list.

## 4 · The rejected archive expires

v1.0.23 made a rejection *parked* rather than deleted so it could be undone. The cost is an
archive that only grows — and a channel re-offers its catalogue every 30 minutes.

After 30 days the record is purged for real: delete + deny tombstone, the same thing
"מחק לצמיתות" does. **That is irreversible**, and for a video inside a channel there is no
way back at all (only a sheet re-add revokes a tombstone, and a channel video has no row).
So the archive states the rule and every row carries its own countdown, both from the same
pure `plan.planRejectedPurge`.

Two safety choices: a record with **no `rejectedAt` is never auto-purged**, and a **nonsense
window falls back to the default** — the first version clamped a negative value to a
one-day window, so a config typo would have emptied the archive on the next sync.

## 5 · Sharing from YouTube said nothing, whatever happened

Reported from the field: sharing a link from the YouTube app does not add it, while pasting
the same link in the parent screen works.

**The native side was never at fault** — intent-filter present, `onNewIntent` covers cold
and warm starts, field names match across the boundary, the event is retained until
consumed. **Nor was the classifier**, checked against the real YouTube-app payloads
including the `?si=` parameter it now appends.

`handleShare` had **seven silent `return`s and no success message either**. Added,
parked-for-approval, duplicate, previously-deleted, cancelled and unsupported all looked
identical from the outside: nothing. "Sharing does not work" was unanswerable from inside
the app, which is exactly why it went unexplained for so long.

- Every route now ends in a **reason**; pure `plan.shareOutcome` turns it into text;
  `ui/toast.js` — the app's first non-blocking feedback channel — shows it.
- **Three real drops**, all in the channel path: a `null` decision (which
  `handleShareInteractive` returns for a channel whenever the PIN screen or a modal is
  already up — easy to hit on a share into a running app), a profile with no `sources`
  record, and a failed resolve reported to a handler that displayed nothing.
- **A shared playlist works too** — `classifySourceRow` understood playlists since v1.0.12
  but `classifyShared` never did. A watch link merely carrying `&list=` stays a video.
- **The profile question is asked whenever there is a choice.** `shouldAskShareProfile` is
  deleted rather than left as an unused export.

⚠️ **Still unconfirmed:** the exact field failure could not be reproduced without a device.
Every drop identifiable with certainty is fixed, and the feedback is the permanent
diagnostic — if it still fails, the app now names the reason.

## Testing

**379 → 388 tests.** Every new guard was proven to fail on a planted regression — and one
of them only became real because of that discipline: the "no silent share route" guard
first anchored to the start of a line, so it could not fail on `if (!c) return;`, the exact
shape it exists to catch.

**Two bugs were caught by browser verification while the suite was green**, continuing the
v1.0.25 pattern:

1. **The playlist approval dialog never appeared** even though all 30 videos imported
   correctly. `pendingKeysOfChannel` matched on `channelId`, which for a playlist video is
   deliberately the OWNER channel — so looking up the playlist id found zero. That is the
   exact shape of the v1.0.22 bug on a new path.
2. **The preview bubble's selection preservation did not work** when done by parameter:
   `refreshAfterAdd` rebuilds the list a beat later and cleared the ticks back.

A third was caught by a test the feature brought with it: the `Math.max(1, …)` window clamp.

See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for what the suite structurally cannot see, and for the device
checklist — several items in this release (the share intent, the per-profile kiosk lock,
cross-device convergence) have **never** been verified on real hardware.

No breaking changes. Nothing resets, nothing needs re-configuring.
